# Penny — MVP feature list (draft)

## 1. Getting started

- Sign in with a Google account.
- A short first-time setup: add your first student, add their lesson schedule, and land on a finished "you're set up" screen.

## 2. Home

The first screen a tutor sees each time they open Penny.

- A short list of things that need attention (e.g. a lesson to confirm, a payment to chase).
- A look at this week's lessons at a glance.
- What's coming up next.
- A small calendar for quick orientation.

## 3. Lessons and schedule

- See your lessons by week or by month.
- See each lesson's status (e.g. scheduled, cancelled, make-up).
- Create, edit, and cancel lessons.

## 4. Students

- See a list of your students.
- Open a student's profile.
- Add/edit student.

## 5. Payments

- See a list of payments.
- Record a payment against a student.
- See a payment's status (e.g. paid, due, overdue).

## 6. Account

- View your tutor profile.
- Sign out.
- Edit profile.

## 7. Shareable pages and invoicing

- Public, no-login page you can share with a parent showing the tutee details, lesson schedule, plus what they owe.
- Invoices as a document.
